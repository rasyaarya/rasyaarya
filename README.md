## About

I am a third-semester student in Artificial Intelligence Engineering at ITS, Indonesia. My primary focus is data science and data mining, with a strong interest in signed graphs, opinion polarization, and building interactive web dashboards. I enjoy turning coursework into working, hands-on projects that are actually usable.

Beyond coursework, I like exploring the full pipeline: from raw data and graph algorithms to a polished, deployed interface. Most of my work sits between analysis and engineering.

## Education

- **Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)** — B.S. in Artificial Intelligence Engineering (ongoing, Semester 3)
- **Department of Informatics** — Faculty of Intelligent Electrical and Informatics Technology (FTEIC)

## Focus Areas

- Data science and data mining: preprocessing, classification, clustering, anomaly detection
- Graph theory: signed directed graphs, PageRank variants, Heider balance
- Web engineering: React, Three.js, interactive dashboards, client-side computation
- Robotics basics: ROS 2, TurtleSim control
